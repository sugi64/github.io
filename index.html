Otong Uploader

A personal, self-hosted utility for publishing videos to its owner’s own YouTube channel.

What this app does

Otong Uploader is a small tool used by a single person (its owner) to publish videos that he has produced himself to his own YouTube channel. It connects to the YouTube Data API v3 with the owner’s authorization and performs only the actions the owner explicitly asks for:

  • uploading a video file from the owner’s computer or private server to his own channel;
  • setting the video’s title, description, tags, category and privacy status;
  • updating the thumbnail and visibility of his own videos.

Who uses it

The app is not distributed to the public and is not offered as a service. It runs on the owner’s own machines and is used only by the owner (fewer than 100 users). There is no sign-up, no user database and no third-party audience.

How it authorizes

The owner signs in with his own Google Account through Google’s standard OAuth 2.0 authorization screen and grants the YouTube scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl. The resulting OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner’s computer and private server, never on a public service. Otong Uploader has no server-side backend that collects or stores data from anyone else.
